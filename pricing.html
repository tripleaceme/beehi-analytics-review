Good to know

Your pricing questions,
answered.

Clear scope. Clear costs. No guesswork.

What is included in setup?

We review your records and measurement requirements, agree the reporting scope and configure your analytics service. Once setup is ready, we walk you through using it.

Why is the first payment different from the monthly rate?

Your first payment includes your selected subscription period plus a one-time setup fee. Starter has a six-month minimum, Growth three months and Business one month.

Is data entry included?

Data entry and additional cleanup are quoted separately. If you need help digitising notebooks or organising records, select the data-entry option during signup.

Do I need to book a call before signing up?

No. Tell us what you want to measure, choose your plan and submit your payment receipt. We review your requirements and confirm the setup scope and timeline.

Can I change my plan later?

Contact us to discuss the additional scope, costs and delivery timing before changing your plan.

Need help choosing a plan?

Tell us about your records and the questions you want answered. We’ll clarify which plan covers your requirements.
