BeeHi Analytics is an Analytics-as-a-Service (AaaS) business helping SMEs transform their business data into actionable insights for better decision-making.

Our Mission

Make business intelligence accessible to every SME.

Nigeria had an estimated 39.7 million micro, small and medium enterprises, according to IFC’s November 2022 report. These businesses contributed 46.32% of GDP in the NBS/SMEDAN 2021 survey, cited in PwC’s 2024 report. Behind those figures are owners who need to understand their sales, costs and payments.

Useful analytics takes more than software. It needs reliable data, relevant business questions, analysis and ongoing care. Many SMEs need those capabilities but cannot justify building and maintaining an internal team.

Your analytics capability, delivered as a service. Developed by Behind The Data Ltd, BeeHi Analytics brings together data integration, analysis, reporting, hosting and ongoing support. You gain business intelligence without the cost and complexity of building an internal analytics team.

Why We Exist

Turn everyday records into answers you can use.

Recording a sale is one step. Knowing how sales are changing, which customers still owe you and where the money goes takes a clearer view.

When those answers are buried in rows of data, it is easy to miss an overdue payment or an order that needs attention.

We start with the decisions you need to make, then connect your records and analyse the patterns behind performance. Dashboards are one interface for those insights; the service also includes ongoing technical support and review conversations where included in your plan.

Our story

It started with one business in Oshogbo.

We built a dashboard for Olamide Craft, a fashion business in Oshogbo, Osun State. His order, payment and customer records became a clearer view of what was happening in the business.

With the dashboard on his phone, Olamide could follow orders, check payments and see the figures he needed without working through every record each time.

The value was in understanding the business and knowing what to do next. That raised a bigger question: how many more owners could benefit from the same analytical support?

That question became BeeHi Analytics. From Oshogbo to Lagos, Ibadan, Abuja and beyond, our ambition is to help more business owners across Nigeria and Africa understand the records they already keep.
