Before you get started

A few things you may be wondering.

It is ongoing access to analytics capabilities without maintaining an internal team. BeeHi Analytics brings together data integration, analysis, reporting, hosting and support. Your dashboard is one way you use the service; review calls and additional analysis depend on your plan.

You need to know what you want to measure and keep your business records up to date. We handle the technical setup and walk you through using your dashboard when it is ready.

You can start with the records you already have. Tell us what format they are in when you sign up. We will review what needs organising and quote separately for cleanup or data entry where needed.

Complete signup with your records, measurement goals, plan and payment. We verify the payment, review your requirements and confirm the setup scope and timeline. Timing depends on data readiness and the analysis you need.

Your reporting uses the business records connected during setup. Standard dashboards refresh daily, with custom schedules available by plan. You, your staff or our separately priced data-entry service keep the source records up to date.

Yes. Access your private dashboard in a browser on your phone, tablet or computer. Dedicated Windows, macOS, iPhone and Android apps are planned. Browser access is available now.

Choose a plan and billing period during signup to see your total, including setup. Payment is by bank transfer; submit your proof of payment through the signup form. No card is required.

Your dashboard is password protected. Keep your login and source-data sharing settings limited to authorised people. We agree how your business records are connected and managed during setup.

Access ends when your paid subscription period expires. Starter and Growth have minimum initial billing periods, shown above. Your source records remain yours.

Yes. Talk to us on WhatsApp about the questions you need answered. We can help you choose a plan or discuss an upgrade, its cost and the delivery timing.
