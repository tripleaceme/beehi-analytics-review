The essentials

Know how your commission works.

What earns commission?

A customer signs up with your code and makes their first confirmed payment. Introductions and demo requests alone do not qualify.

What gets paid?

15% of that first payment, including subscription and setup. This is a one-time commission, not a share of future renewals.

When is it paid?

Bank transfer on the last business day of each month. Balances below ₦5,000 carry forward to the next payout run.
