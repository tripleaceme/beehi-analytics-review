The service journey

What happens next.

01

Share your business goals

Tell us what you want to measure, how your business works and which records you keep. Detailed goals are required so we can assess the right scope.

02

Choose your plan and pay

Select a plan and billing period, review the full total including setup, then transfer and submit your payment receipt.

03

Review the scope together

We verify payment, review your records and confirm the measures, source connections and timeline. Additional data preparation is quoted separately.

04

Build and check your analytics

We organise the agreed data, configure the reporting and check the calculations. Your dashboard is built around the questions and scope agreed for your plan.

05

Get ready to use it

Once the service is ready, we walk you through your dashboard and what you need to keep updated. Your private login gives you access from a phone or computer.

06

Keep the insight useful

Maintain your source records while we support the reporting. Standard refresh is daily; review calls, priority support and custom schedules depend on your plan.

Working together

Good analysis starts with useful records.

01

You bring the context

Existing records, the measures you need, relevant access and someone who can answer questions about your business.

02

We bring the analytics

Source configuration, reporting setup, calculation checks, dashboard hosting and the support included in your subscription.

03

We agree the boundaries

Record quality affects setup time. Data entry, extra cleanup and work beyond your plan are discussed and scoped separately.
